Good to know

When are you open?

Every day, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do you have veg options?

Yes. Fresh Veggie, Deluxe Veggie, Veggie Paradise, Peppy Paneer, Indi Tandoori Paneer, Veg Extra Vaganza and Cheese N Corn are all vegetarian.

Can I sit on the rooftop?

Yes. Mention it in your reservation and we will try our best to keep a rooftop table for you.

Can I add extra cheese?

Yes, for ₹75 on any pizza.
